Copy this into the ticket. It is the course's own aid (lesson [A troubleshooting method](/course/troubleshooting/trb-method)). Tick items as you collect them; write the **time** next to every observation.

## Before you look

- [ ] Ticket text in the user's words, the time it started and who reported it.
- [ ] <Term id="symptom">Symptom</Term> written without a cause.
- [ ] <Term id="ts-scope">Scope</Term>: one user, one Edge, one pair of sites, or all. Who is not affected?
- [ ] What changed recently, and when?

## Read-only evidence, in order

- [ ] Edge status (Connected or not) and its Profile and release.
- [ ] **Monitor > Events** around the start time: names, times, which component generated them.
- [ ] The **Monitor > Edges** tab for the layer: Links, Paths, QoE or Flows.
- [ ] The one read-only <Term id="remote-diagnostics">Remote Diagnostics</Term> test that answers the question left (List Paths, VPN Test, Route Table Dump, List Active Flows, Interface Status, NTP Dump).
- [ ] The same evidence from the other side of the problem (the other Edge, the Hub, the Gateway).

## Reasoning

- [ ] One hypothesis, and the observation that would disprove it.
- [ ] The test you ran and its result.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Do not change state to explore">
  Flush, clear, restart, reboot and failover actions change state. Do them only under a documented procedure or Support's direction.
</Callout>

## After the change

- [ ] The one change you made, and where in the VCO.
- [ ] The same evidence read again, and the time.
- [ ] A note for the next engineer (see the [support case template](/troubleshooting/support-case-template)).
