The text below is **this course's own template**, not an Arista form. The bundles are the ones the Troubleshooting Guide documents (lesson [Escalation and documentation](/course/troubleshooting/trb-escalate)).

## Case summary

```text
Symptom (user's words):
Started (UTC):
Scope (affected / not affected):
Recent changes:
Edge(s), Profile, release:

Evidence (time, screen or test, result):
  1.
  2.
  3.

Hypotheses tried and refuted:
Current hypothesis:

Attached: <Term id="diagnostic-bundle">diagnostic bundle</Term> (Operators request it), <Term id="packet-capture-bundle">packet capture bundle</Term>
What we need from Support:
```

## Packet capture bundle: before you submit

- A target Edge and an interface (or VLAN), and a short duration in seconds.
- At least one filter if you want filtering: IP1, IP2, IP1:Port1, IP2:Port2 or Protocol.
- A reason for generation.

## Post-incident note

```text
What happened (one paragraph):
Root cause and the evidence that shows it:
What was changed, where in the VCO, and by whom:
How it was verified (same evidence, time):
What to watch for:
```

<Callout variant="warning" title="Packet captures can hold customer traffic">
  Use a filter and a short duration, and share the bundle only through the documented route.
</Callout>
