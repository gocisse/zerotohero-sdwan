## Permission, not treatment

Business policy answers *how should this traffic be treated?* (priority, link, service). The firewall answers *may this traffic pass?*. Both match traffic and both exist at Profile and Edge level, which is why they get confused.

## Rule structure

A firewall rule matches parameters such as IP addresses, ports, VLAN IDs, interfaces, MAC addresses, domain names, protocols (GRE, ICMP, TCP, UDP) and applications; an application match depends on the <Term id="dpi" /> engine. Its **action** is:

| Action | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Allow** | permits the packets |
| **Drop** | drops silently, no notice to the source |
| **Reject** | drops and tells the source with a reset |
| **Skip** | skips the rule and processes the next one |

Reject and Skip are available only when **Stateful Firewall** is activated.

## Stateful behaviour

A stateful firewall enforces symmetric routing and matching is **directional**: you can allow VLAN 1 hosts to start sessions to VLAN 2 and deny the reverse. Asymmetric routing is not supported on Edges with it activated. By default the Orchestrator deactivates Stateful Firewall for an Enterprise.

<Callout variant="gotcha" title="Inbound traffic is blocked by default">
  By default the Edge blocks all inbound traffic except self-destined traffic, unless you configure Port Forwarding or 1:1 NAT (<Term id="inbound-acl">Inbound ACLs</Term>, under Configure > Edges > Firewall > Additional Settings). Edge firewalls apply to traffic passing through the Edge, not to self-originating or self-destined traffic.
</Callout>

## Profile, Edge and logging

Edges inherit the firewall rules, security settings and Stateful Firewall settings from the Profile. Profile rules cannot be updated at the Edge, and **Edge override rules take priority**. Logs: the Edge can send firewall logs to the Orchestrator and to a syslog <Term id="collector">collector</Term>, and you view them at **Monitor > Firewall Logs**.

## What you would see in the VCO

- **Configure > Profiles > Firewall** (Configure Firewall, Firewall Rules, + NEW RULE) and **Configure > Edges > (Edge) > Firewall** with an **Override** checkbox.
- **Monitor > Firewall Logs**.

## Key takeaways

- Firewall = permission (Allow, Drop, Reject, Skip); business policy = treatment.
- Stateful Firewall is off by default; it makes matching directional and enables Reject and Skip.
- Inbound traffic is blocked by default unless Port Forwarding or 1:1 NAT is set.
- Edge override rules beat Profile rules.

## What would you look for in the VCO

- Is Stateful Firewall on for this customer?
- Is there an Edge-level firewall rule above or instead of the Profile rule?
- What do the Firewall Logs say for the blocked flow?
