**By the end of this lesson you will be able to:**

- describe the second Silver variant and where the Edge replaces a device;
- separate three kinds of redundancy: router redundancy (<Term id="hsrp">HSRP</Term> or <Term id="vrrp">VRRP</Term>), an <Term id="edge-ha">Edge HA pair</Term>, and the Edge's own VRRP option;
- walk through what is documented about an Edge HA failover;
- say what the Edge does and does not do for each failure.

## What the vendor says

The second Silver variant has **MPLS and Internet routers deployed with HSRP or VRRP**, with an **L2 switch on the LAN side**. The Edge **replaces the L2 switch**. (Older text names only HSRP; the 7.0 text names HSRP or VRRP.)

<Diagram id="top-silver-2" />

The vendor's text read does not show a pair of Edges in this variant and does not say how the Edge interacts with the HSRP or VRRP between the two routers.

## Three kinds of redundancy

| Kind | What provides it | What the documentation says |
|---|---|---|
| Router redundancy | the two routers, with HSRP or VRRP between them | the variant names them; the protocol is the routers' own (general networking, not Edge behaviour) |
| Edge redundancy | an <Term id="active-standby">Active/Standby</Term> Edge HA pair | documented in detail below |
| Edge VRRP | the Edge as VRRP primary with a third-party router | an Edge option; guidelines documented for older releases |

Two points that are easy to mix up:

- On an Edge HA pair with L2 and L3 switches (the HA guide's Deployment Type 2), **HSRP or VRRP is required on the L3 switch pair**. It runs on the switches. The Edge does not run it for them.
- The Edge's own VRRP option is a different feature: High Availability on the Device tab offers None, Active Standby Pair, Cluster and **VRRP with 3rd party router**. The older guide says VRRP can only run between the Edge and a third-party router on the same subnet through an L2 switch, that the Edge must have the higher priority to be primary, that it cannot be combined with Active-Standby HA, and that it is not supported on an interface with a WAN overlay. We could not read the 7.0 page of these guidelines, so check them in your release.

## Edge HA as documented

- **Models.** Standard HA (same configuration, symmetric connections to the same WAN links), Enhanced HA (each Edge has its own WAN links) and a mixed mode.
- **Requirements.** Two Edges of the **same model**; directly connected, with under 100 ms latency; Arista does not recommend an L2 switch on the HA link; in Standard HA the LAN-side switches must support and run STP, and the Edges need mirrored WAN and LAN connections.
- **What is synchronised.** The Orchestrator talks to the Active Edge and its configuration is mirrored to the Standby over the failover link; session information is synchronised; with BGP, the local routes are synchronised.
- **Failover triggers.** A WAN or LAN link failure on the Active Edge, the Active Edge crashing, rebooting or becoming unresponsive (the Standby receives no heartbeat messages), a configuration change that restarts the service, and loss of signal detection on routed interfaces. The default failover threshold is 700 ms.
- **What happens.** The Standby takes over the Active role with minimal impact on traffic and the Orchestrator raises the **High Availability Going Active** event.
- **Which Edge is Active.** The one with more LAN interfaces; WAN interfaces break a tie.
- **Split brain.** If the pair cannot see each other, both may become Active. Legacy HA uses a layer 2 heartbeat; in Enhanced HA the Primary Gateway is the only detection method.

## Failures, and what the Edge does about each

| Failure | Who responds | Status |
|---|---|---|
| An Internet or MPLS router in an HSRP or VRRP pair fails | its standby router | general protocol behaviour; what the Edge does is not documented |
| A WAN link of a single Edge fails | DMPO steers to other links | documented (Section 3) |
| A WAN link of the Active Edge in an HA pair fails | the HA page lists it as a failover trigger; how it interacts with DMPO steering to other links is not documented | partly documented |
| A LAN link of the Active Edge fails | failover to the Standby if it has a working LAN link | documented |
| The only Edge fails | the site is cut off; the vendor warns that an in-path Edge is a single point of failure that may need HA | documented warning |
| The Active Edge of an HA pair fails | the Standby takes over, event raised | documented |
| An L2 or L3 switch fails | not documented beyond the STP and HSRP or VRRP requirements for the HA designs | not documented |

</Callout>

## What you would see in the VCO

- **Configure > Edges** > the Edge > **Device** > **High Availability**: **None**, **Active Standby Pair**, **Cluster** or **VRRP with 3rd party router**; Advanced Options hold the HA Failover Detection Time Multiplier and Pre-empt Switchover.
- **Monitor > Edges**: an **HA State** that turns green when the pair is ready, and an **HA Standby** tab.
- **Monitor > Events**: **High Availability Going Active** and loss of signal events.
- **Remote Actions** from the Edge's Shortcuts menu include **Force HA Failover** (use it only in a lab).

<Callout variant="tip" title="Check this in your own VCO or site">
  - **[observation candidate]** Open: Configure > Edges, an Edge, Device tab, High Availability section. Expect: the choices None, Active Standby Pair, Cluster and VRRP with 3rd party router. Docs may differ: a 6.x release may list different choices.
  - **[observation candidate]** Open: Monitor > Edges on an HA pair. Expect: an HA State (green when ready) and an HA Standby tab. Docs may differ: how the Standby appears in the list is not documented.
  - **[observation candidate]** In a lab only: Remote Actions > Force HA Failover, then Monitor > Events. Expect: a High Availability Going Active event. Docs may differ: the event name or the time it takes.
  - **[observation candidate]** Open: the HA Advanced Options. Expect: the failover threshold is 700 ms by default. Docs may differ: the field is a multiplier and your release may show a different default.
  - **[observation candidate]** Open: the routers' HSRP or VRRP status (on the routers). Expect: one active router per pair while the Edge sits between them and the LAN. Docs may differ: the vendor text does not say how the Edge interacts with it.
</Callout>

## Key takeaways

- Silver variant 2: HSRP or VRRP routers, an L2 switch on the LAN side, the Edge replacing the L2 switch.
- Router redundancy, an Edge HA pair and Edge VRRP are three different things.
- HSRP or VRRP on the L3 switch pair is required in the HA design with L2 and L3 switches.
- An HA failover is triggered by Edge-side failures and raises High Availability Going Active.

## What would you look for in the VCO

- What does the High Availability section say, and is the HA State green?
- Are the two Edges the same model?
- Which events did the last failover raise?
