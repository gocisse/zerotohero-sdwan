**By the end of this lesson you will be able to:**

- decide when to stop investigating and escalate;
- request a <Term id="diagnostic-bundle">diagnostic bundle</Term> and a <Term id="packet-capture-bundle">packet capture bundle</Term> and say who can;
- write a case summary that a stranger can act on;
- write a short post-incident note.

## When to escalate

Escalate when the read-only <Term id="evidence">evidence</Term> does not explain the symptom, when the next step is state-changing, or when a documented threshold says to. The ARP Table Dump procedure ends with "collect the Diagnostic bundle and the Packet Capture bundle and contact Arista Customer Support"; the Gateway thresholds say to contact Support at a critical alarm. Anything beyond those statements is **our method**.

## What a bundle needs

| Item | Edge | Gateway |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Diagnostics tab > Diagnostic Bundles | Operator portal > Gateway Management > Diagnostic Bundles |
| Packet capture bundle | Target, Interface (or VLAN), Duration in seconds, Reason for Generation (optional), PCAP Filters, Advanced Filters | Target, Connectivity (interface or Edge ID), Duration (default 5 seconds), Reason, filters |
| Diagnostic bundle | Target, Reason for Generation, Core Limit | Target, Reason, Core Limit |
| Who | PCAP bundle: Operators, Standard Admins, Customer Support; diagnostic bundle: Operators only | Operator Superuser or Admin; a Partner with Gateway management access |
| After | More > Download CSV, then send the bundle to Support | Download Bundle as ZIP; bundles are deleted on their Cleanup Date |

The Edge's diagnostic bundle is the "collection of all the configuration and logs from a specific Edge"; the Core Limit option reduces the size when Internet connectivity has issues. PCAP filters take IP1, IP2, IP1:Port1, IP2:Port2 or Protocol, and at least one filter is needed to use filtering. Capturing the HA interface of a standby Edge is documented from release 5.2.0.

<Callout variant="info" title="Capture only what you need">
  A packet capture can hold customer traffic. Use a filter and a short duration, and share the bundle only with Support through the documented route.
</Callout>

## A case summary (our template)

The page [support case template](/troubleshooting/support-case-template) has the full text. In short:

1. One-line symptom and start time (UTC).
2. Scope: who is affected and who is not.
3. What changed recently.
4. The evidence collected, with times and the tests read.
5. Hypotheses tried and refuted.
6. Bundles attached, and the Edge release.
7. What you need from Support.

## A post-incident note (our template)

After the fix, write: what happened, the root cause with the evidence that shows it, what was changed and where, how it was verified, and what to watch for. Keep it factual; it is the first thing the next engineer reads.

## What you would see in the VCO

- **Diagnostics > Diagnostic Bundles** with Request PCAP Bundle and Request Diagnostic Bundle, and the status of each request.
- **More > Download CSV** on the bundles list.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Check this in your own VCO">
  - **[observation candidate]** Open: Diagnostics > Diagnostic Bundles for an Edge. Expect: Request PCAP Bundle and Request Diagnostic Bundle buttons according to your role. Docs may differ: your role.
  - **[observation candidate]** Open: a PCAP request dialog (do not submit). Expect: Target, Interface or VLAN, Duration, Reason and PCAP Filters fields. Docs may differ: field labels.
  - **[observation candidate]** Open: the diagnostic bundle list after a request in a lab. Expect: a status that goes to complete and a Cleanup Date. Docs may differ: the status names.
</Callout>

## Key takeaways

- Escalate when evidence does not explain it, when the next step is state-changing, or when a threshold says so.
- Bundles: Edge diagnostic bundle (Operators), PCAP bundle (Operators, Standard Admins, Customer Support), with filters and short durations.
- A good case summary lists scope, changes, evidence, refuted hypotheses and the release.
- The templates are ours, not Arista's.

## What would you look for in the VCO

- Which role do I have, and can I request the bundle?
- What filter and duration will capture the problem and no more?
- Does my case summary let a stranger act on it?
